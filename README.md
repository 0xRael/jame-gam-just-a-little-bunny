## Just a Little Bunny... Right?

_Created for Jame Gam's game Jam (Theme: "I'm just a little guy")_

Your brother is going on a work vacation for 3 days and he asks you to take care of his bunny while he’s away. You of course accept but he tells you to take VERY good care of him, along with a set of instructions on how to take care of him.
But I'm sure it will be fine! He's just a little bunny, Isn't he...?

### Play the Game on [Itch.io](https://darthrael.itch.io/its-just-a-small-bunny-right)!
